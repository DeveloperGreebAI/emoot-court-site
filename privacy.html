Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

This policy explains what information E-Moot Court, operated by GreenAI ("we"), uses when you use the E-Moot Court web app and its Google Meet™ add-on, why, and the choices you have.

Information we use

Information Where it comes from Why
Name and email address Your Nexus sign-in and your institution's invitation To identify you and link you to your institution
Your institution, role, team and hearing seats Your institution's E-Moot Court administrator To show you only your hearings and the tools of your role
The Google account email used in Meet Your institution's administrator, or you To invite the right account to the hearing's Meet room
Google Meet meeting code The Google Meet add-on platform, when you open the add-on To find the E-Moot hearing that belongs to the call
Hearing content: timer, marks, team chat messages, your private notes You and the other participants To run the hearing and keep the competition record
Hearing transcript Created by E-Moot Court's transcription service when the institution runs the hearing with a transcript and participants have accepted the transcript notice For judging, feedback and the competition record
Technical logs (time, address, browser, errors) Your browser Security, preventing abuse and fixing problems

What the Google Meet add-on does not do

  • It does not access the call's audio, video, chat or participant list.
  • It does not read your Gmail, Drive, Calendar or other Google data.
  • It reads only the meeting code that Google Meet gives add-ons, to find your hearing.
  • It keeps its sign-in only in memory while the panel is open; it does not store cookies in Meet.

Google user data

To create the Meet rooms of hearings, E-Moot Court uses an organiser Google account of the institution with permission to create Meet meeting spaces. We use that permission only to create and manage the hearings' Meet rooms. E-Moot Court's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not use Google user data for advertising, do not sell it and do not use it to train AI models.

Who can see what

  • Your private notes: only you.
  • Team chat: only the members of your team in that hearing.
  • Transcript: the judges and the teams of the hearing.
  • Marks: the judges; results are shown to teams once the institution publishes them.
  • Your institution's administrators manage the competition and its participants.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run E-Moot Court (hosting, the Nexus sign-in service and the transcription service), under contracts that limit their use to providing that service, or when the law requires it.

Storage, security and retention

Data is stored on servers operated by GreenAI and protected by access controls; each institution's data is kept separate. Hearing records are kept for as long as your institution needs them for its competition record, then deleted. Your institution can ask us to delete its data earlier.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, subject to your institution's record-keeping duties. Contact your institution's administrator or email us at developer@greenai.services. You can stop using the add-on at any time by closing it, and your Google Workspace administrator can remove it.

Changes

We will update this page when the way we use information changes, and change the date above.
