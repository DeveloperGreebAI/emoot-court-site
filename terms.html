The service

E-Moot Court helps institutions run moot court competitions: events, teams, hearing rooms, timers, transcripts, team chat, notes and marks. The Google Meet add-on shows the hearing panel inside a Meet call. The service is currently provided to the greenai.services organisation for testing and may change while it is developed.
