Support

Help for the E-Moot Court add-on for Google Meet™. For questions about a competition (your seat, team, schedule or marks), contact your institution's E-Moot Court administrator first.

Open the add-on

  1. Sign in to Chrome or Meet with your greenai.services Google account.
  2. Join the Google Meet call of your hearing.
  3. Click Activities (bottom right) and choose E-Moot Court. If it is not listed, ask your Google Workspace administrator to install it for your account.
  4. Press Sign in. A small window opens: sign in with your E-Moot Court account if asked, then press Allow. The window closes and the panel shows your hearing.

Common problems

You see What to do
"This Google Meet call is not an E-Moot hearing you are part of." Open the add-on in the Meet call linked to your hearing, signed in with the E-Moot account seated in it. If you switch between institutions, sign in again. Otherwise ask the administrator to check your seat.
The sign-in window does not open Allow pop-up windows for meet.google.com in your browser and press Sign in again.
"The sign-in has expired" Sign-in requests last 5 minutes. Press Sign in again.
"E-Moot Court cannot be reached" Check your internet connection and try again. If it continues, contact support.
A tab is missing (Transcript, Team chat) Tabs depend on your role: the transcript is for judges and teams, team chat only for team members and only while the event allows it.

Contact support

Email developer@greenai.services. Please include your browser, the room code, the step that went wrong and the exact message shown. Never send passwords, sign-in codes or confidential case material.

We usually reply within two working days.
